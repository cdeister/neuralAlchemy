Layer_ :

A photo & science zine series · in preparation

The Idea

The cortex does its work in layers, and so will we. Layer_ is a zine series exploring the juxtapositions of art and science — each title an homage to the function of a cortical layer, which lets the series be broad and deep at the same time.

Layer_V · Flagship

Integrative and free-form, grounded in photography and science — the way layer V integrates across the column and carries its output abroad. Layer_V will showcase the range of the other Layer_ approaches.

FIRST ISSUE — “LAYER_V: CHILDHOOD SCALES” — IN PREPARATION

Layer_IV

As close to a pure art zine as possible — the way layer IV relays nearly pure peripheral sensory signal into the cortex. Minimal commentary; the work arrives unprocessed.

Layer_I

Highly conceptual — abstract pieces and ideas about ideas, the way layer I carries contextual, top-down information over everything beneath it.
