Internal · Mark Explorations · Rounds 2–4

Orientation studies, runners-up, syntheses

Working document — unlisted. For critique, not publication.

A · The triangle question

Response to the AA/illuminati read. The gestalt is real — triangle-in-circle is one of the oldest containment figures we have, which is exactly why it works. The square and the filled soma already push our mark away from both references (AA has no square; the pyramid has an eye, not a body). But orientation changes the reading entirely — in alchemy, △ is fire and ▽ is water.

B · The runners-up, rendered

Directions 2–5 from Round 1, drawn instead of described — for critique and for the record. (▷ apex-right was tested and cut without ceremony: it's a play button.)

C · Syntheses — R2.7 × R2.8

The retort crossed with the billboard fragment: alchemy's vessel drawn at Rosenquist scale, with pop-art machinery (halftone, seam, crop) doing alchemical work. Four attempts, each keeping one rule from each parent.

D · Round 4 — The synapse in the picture

Chris saw it first: the flask was already a neuron — a bulb for a soma, a spout for a process. Round 4 makes that identity structural. If the retort is a cell, the drop is a vesicle, and everything the studio releases is transmission. Behind the billboard, a more primitive layer: pyramidal pictographs — the older poster showing through the paste. And in every frame the same rule: the dendrite never touches the drop. The gap is the point.

E · The Pour — refined, and printed

You called it: R3.4 wants to be worn. Two refinements first — the vessel now visibly tips into the pour (a tilted meniscus line inside the bulb), and the letters tumble on a truer ballistic arc, led by two small beads of liquid. Then the colorways.
