Investigator · 001

Chris Deister, Ph.D.

Founding Investigator · Neuroscientist & Designer

Pursuit

A lifetime pursuit: to derive — and contribute to — theories of nervous system design and function.

Research

  • Forebrain design and function How the front of the brain is organized, and why.
  • Cortical & basal ganglia circuitry and function The loops that select, sequence, and shape action.
  • AI as an instrument for sensory processing across the neural-spectrum With a particular interest in aesthetics and autism.

Practice

  • Designing tools for ADHD, autism, and related conditions Software and objects that improve daily life for real nervous systems.
  • Generative art & pen-plotter drawing Algorithmic composition, executed in ink.
  • Photography An ongoing study of light, attention, and place.

Elsewhere

  • github.com/cdeister Code, instruments, and works in progress.
