What · The Studio

Research, Art, Studio — three primitives, one practice.

The Idea

The market funds the inquiry; the inquiry sharpens the work.

Neural Alchemy is an academically minded design studio. Our goal is structural: enable artists and investigators to support themselves — and their long-term research and art — by bringing their day-to-day creative work-products to market.

The Model

The studio is organized around investigators — artists and scientists who each carry a lifetime question alongside a working design practice. An investigator might ship an app in the morning, run a consulting engagement in the afternoon, and plot ink drawings at night. All of it is one practice, and each investigator's page is the front door to theirs.

The Work

Applications with a point of view. Neuroscience-literate consulting. Generative and pen-plotter art, photography, and printed editions. Product and furniture design for real nervous systems — including neurodivergent ones. And research instruments: equipment for studying human behavior as it relates to working and producing art.

Learning · In the Works

In the near future we aim to offer hands-on courses and experiences, and we'll post details here as they firm up. Topics will run across the whole bench, built for curious people across age and experience levels:

  • Painting
  • Multi-media art
  • Data science
  • Machine learning
  • Robotics
  • Neuroscience

Lineage

The name is a claim about transformation — base material into something rarer. Our lineage runs through the Bauhaus conviction that the workshop and the seminar belong in the same building, and through Bell Labs and E.A.T., where engineers and artists worked as one shop. We think that's still the best idea anyone has had about how making and knowing fit together.
