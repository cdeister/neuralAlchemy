## Round 1 — The Squared Circle (2026-08-11)

**Brief (Chris):** Simplify. Name + tagline **"Research, Art, Studio"** — the three
words are *primitives that form an integrated whole*. Bauhaus as broad inspiration,
not literal. Neuroscience/computational flair. Art loves: Rosenquist, Ruscha,
Rauschenberg. Approach the logo the way Paul Rand approached NeXT: one argued
solution, not a sheet of options.

**The argument.** There is exactly one visual tradition built on three primitives
forming a whole: the Bauhaus preliminary course — circle, triangle, square. And
exactly one historical diagram that integrates those three forms into a single
figure: the alchemical **squared circle**, the construction for the philosopher's
stone. The company is named Neural Alchemy. The mark was sitting in the name.

**The mark.** A circle (Research — the whole question) circumscribing a triangle
(Art — fire, transformation) circumscribing a square (Studio — the made thing,
the workshop), holding a solid vermilion center. Third reading: triangle + round
red body = **pyramidal neuron with its soma** — the Cajal cell abstracted to pure
geometry. Four registration ticks = the Bell Labs schematic voice. Every element
is one of the three words; nothing is decorative. Geometry is exact (true
inscriptions; tangencies computed).

**Palette:** paper `#f6f3ec` · ink `#16130e` · vermilion `#c8401a` · cobalt
`#1f3a93` (reserve). One Ruscha detail: the tagline's commas are set in vermilion.

**Runners-up (ranked):**
1. The Squared Circle — shipped.
2. The Spike — action-potential oscilloscope trace; strong Bell Labs voice, says
   "neuro" without "alchemy."
3. NA monogram — Rand-style letterplay, synapse at the shared stroke; wit without
   the cosmology.
4. The Retort — distillation vessel, circle + spout + vermilion drop; drifts
   apothecary.
5. The Rosenquist fragment — billboard-crop halftone neuron as masthead art, not a
   mark; steal later for edition posters.

**Client response (Chris):** Feels right; palette reads modern-not-AI and "Austin";
simplicity and fork/node presentation praised. Critique: the triangle-in-circle
carries an AA / illuminati gestalt — taken as a compliment (deep primitive), but
explore triangle orientations. Blurb: keep opening "An academically minded design
studio in Austin, Texas —", sub *scientists* for *investigators*, work *studio*
into the triad. Add an 03 "Layer_ :" node. Drop the WHO/WHAT hint text.
