01

The Studio

Neural Alchemy is an academically minded design firm. We study how nervous systems are built, and we build things the way nervous systems would.

The studio is organized around investigators — partners who each carry a long-term research or artistic pursuit alongside a working design practice. An investigator might ship an app in the morning, run a consulting engagement in the afternoon, and plot ink drawings at night. All of it is one practice.

Our goal is structural: enable investigators to support themselves, and their long-term research and art, by bringing their day-to-day creative work-products to market. The market funds the inquiry; the inquiry sharpens the work.

Practice

  • Applications

    Software with a point of view — tools shaped by how attention, memory, and perception actually work.

  • Consulting

    Neuroscience-literate design and design-literate science, on loan to teams that need either.

  • Art & Editions

    Generative work, pen-plotter drawings, and photography — produced, printed, and sold as editions.

  • Product & Furniture

    Objects for working and making, designed for real nervous systems — including neurodivergent ones.

  • Research Instruments

    Equipment for collecting data on human behavior as it relates to working and producing art.
